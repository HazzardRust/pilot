## Interface

```
CREW_WAIT_MS = 10 * 60_000
crewWaitOf(items, repo, coPilot, now): [{ item, ticket, at }]   // S6's successorOwedOf items, due at postedAt + CREW_WAIT_MS
noteCrewWait(itemId): Item       // one `from: 'deck'` line with a seq; refused when already noted or not due
line: `Successor owed on <ticket>: <item> open 10 min with no co-pilot — deck_ticket co-pilot it and spawn its successor`
```

## Behaviours

1. `a successor owed for 10 minutes wakes the wait` (test): `noteCrewWait` → at postedAt+10m one deck line; a parked `pilot wait` returns with it.
2. `not before, and once` (test): `crewWaitOf` → nothing due at 9m; a second fire is refused.
3. `a grant in time cancels it` (test): `crewWaitOf` → after S5's grant closes the item, nothing is due.

Rewrite check: a polling sweep instead of a timer keeps 1-3, because they read only the item's lines and the wait.
Misses: a pilot session that is not parked in `pilot wait`; S6's SessionStart line covers the next start.
