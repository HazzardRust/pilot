# architect-deeper: the architect works deeper, not wider

Ticket `itm_3885405a5b70` · base `origin/pilot` 1506373f · design branch `design/architect-deeper`

## 1. Problem

The user wants some of the philosophy in Felix Rieseberg's essay "How to be more ambitious: a tutorial for humans
with models" (felixrieseberg.com, 2026-10-13) built into the system, and judged the architect the right place for
it. The essay names four kinds of ambition: speed, altitude, depth and range. The ticket keeps **depth**, meaning
working the way a top expert would, with their instruments, unreasonable targets, challenged assumptions and an
outside judge. It also keeps range's **red team**, meaning attacking the work by doing what an attacker does, not
by role-play. Altitude's "take the human out of the loop" and a general push to be ambitious are out. The
architect goes deeper on the stated ask and never wider.

## 4. Ubiquitous language

- **Bar**: a numeral, a number word or a bound phrase in a spec line, outside code and identifiers, that a user
  could check.
- **Practitioner tag**: `practitioner: <who>` on a §2 line the depth step produced.
- **Red-team line**: a line starting or containing `red team:` in §6 or §8, recording what the attack found or
  what it attacked.
- **Depth step**: the 3 questions of `## Depth first`.
- **Red-team pass**: the attack on the cut after the deepening pass, done by walking the red steps, not by
  role-play.

  blocked-yellow-yields §2).
