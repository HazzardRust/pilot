## Files owned

- `agents/pilot.md`: "Ending a run" — human steps go to `deck_status` `step` as crew reports name them; the `Findings:` reply carries findings only; the steps thread is the user's, never closed by the pilot.
- `agents/pilot-lander.md`: the hand-off line names the steps thread id beside the report.
- `deck/items-bounds.mjs`: `REPORT_DUE` asks for findings only.
- `hooks/test/inbox-alert.test.js`: the `REPORT_DUE` pin.
- `deck/test/report-due.test.mjs`: the `Human steps: none.` pin goes.
- `scripts/test/measure-prompt.test.mjs`: the pilot's prompt pin moves by the measured cost.
