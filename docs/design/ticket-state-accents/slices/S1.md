## Tests

1. `a live co-pilot's yellow ticket is co-pilot-held and labelled`: only that row, never `in-hand`, and its fold carries `open`.
2. `release clears the mark`: no `co-pilot-held` after `setCoPilot(id, false)`.
3. `a co-pilot entry on a ticket now red or closed marks nothing`.
4. `the main slot's in-hand and next are unchanged beside a co-pilot`: the green row keeps `in-hand` (amber) and `next`.
5. `a yellow ticket with no co-pilot carries no mark`.
6. `another repository's co-pilot marks nothing on this tab`.
