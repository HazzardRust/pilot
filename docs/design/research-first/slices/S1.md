## Interface

```
skills/grilling/SKILL.md  "Finding _facts_" paragraph
  research when the default rests on: practice outside the repository; a library's, tool's or service's current API;
    a security, privacy, data or licensing rule; a standard the user named; the user asking
  skip for: a codebase fact; a preference; a scope call; a locked decision or an ADR; research already on the thread
  body line: "Research: <what was read>, <n> sources, favours <option>"
  overturned default: one reply on the thread, the question again with the new default
agents/pilot-explorer.md  ## A research brief
  brief fields: Decision, Options, Practice, Fetch limit (default 8 web fetches; stop at three agreeing sources)
  returns: | Practice | What it does | Source | Implies for <option> ... |, unfetched rows marked, then which option practice favours
  reports the table to its owner only (SendMessage or its final report), never on the thread; the owner may quote a source or row by judgement
measure({ root, home }) -> rows; the pilot and explorer own shares read against their raised pins
agents/pilot.md §3  "research first, then recommend": a pilot-explorer research brief before the question goes up
```
