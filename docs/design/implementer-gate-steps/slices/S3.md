## Tests

2. `QA gates the suite past the 120 s default`: `## The read-only pass` → names `run_in_background`, `600000` and reading the log.
3. `QA's pin moves by exactly the clauses`: `QA_PIN` → the number equals `node scripts/measure-prompt.mjs`'s QA own share, under a history line naming this slice and its delta.

Rewrite check: rewording either clause keeps the three results while the pinned phrases and the measured number hold.
