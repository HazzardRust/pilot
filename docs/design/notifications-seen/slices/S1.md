## Tests

1. `one press clears every notice on the deck`: `POST /clear-notices` → 303 to `back`; `noticesOf(listItems())` is `[]`; the store equals the store before with only `notice` removed.
2. `the press is refused off the deck's own page`: `POST /clear-notices` → 403 for a foreign and for a missing Origin; the notices are kept.
3. `clearing none writes nothing`: `clearAllNotices` runs bare, and `deck.json`'s mtime and bytes are unchanged.
4. `the panel opens with Mark all seen only when it has a row`: `renderStrips` → the `.mark-all` form first with one row; absent on an empty deck.
5. `Mark all seen marks every listed thread at its own count`: `install` → after a `.mark-all` click every `[data-thread]` is hidden, the key maps each id to its `data-said`, and `fetched` is `[]`.
6. `a pilot write after the press lights that thread alone`: `install` → after a swap raising one line's `data-said`, only that line shows and the count is 1.
7. `the notices' share stays until the swap removes them`: `install` → after the click the count is the `[data-notice]` rows; after a swap with none, there is no `data-count`.

Rewrite check: storing "seen" or notices differently keeps every result above, because each one is read through the route, the served page or the stand-in DOM.
