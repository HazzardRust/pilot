## Behaviours

1. `the queues are the page's` (test): `ticketsView` → the groups, rows and switch states the Tickets tab draws for a fixture.
2. `two quick presses move once` (test): `tickets::Local` → a second arrow press while `moving` is set posts nothing; the ticket moves one place (ticket-making bug).
3. `a switch takes a confirm` (test): `tickets::Local` → play shows Confirm; Confirm posts `Switch`; Cancel on a pending one posts `CancelSwitch`.
4. `folds are kept per deck` (test): `regions::tickets::draw` → an opened fold is in `Prefs.folds` and opens after a restart.

Rewrite check: any panel gating presses on the move in flight keeps 2.
