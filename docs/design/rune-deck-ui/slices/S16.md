## Behaviours

1. `the figures are the page's` (test): `usageView` → the readings `renderUsage` draws for a fixture.
2. `the bands are the page's` (test): `usage::band` → 79 fine, 80 warm, 90 high, 95 critical.
3. `a tab press selects its deck` (test): `regions::tabs::draw` → pressing D2 sets `State.query.run` to its repo.
4. `a refused add deck says why and keeps the path` (test): `tabs::Local` → after `Acted::Refused`, `refused` holds the reason and the typed path stays.

Rewrite check: any header with the page's bands and tab addresses keeps 2-3.
