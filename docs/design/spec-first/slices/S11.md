1. `every absolute in the six files is listed and tagged` (read): `git grep -nwiE 'never|always|must'` over the six files → each hit has a row in `absolutes.md` tagged gate, safety, destructive or secret, 0 unlisted.
2. `each kept absolute carries its reason` (read): every listed row's reason sits in the same or the next sentence of the definition.
3. `no definition grows` (run): `node scripts/measure-prompt.mjs` → each of the six own shares at or under its `origin/pilot` (a3b7d463) value; architect headroom at least 100 under its unchanged pin.
4. `references and pins hold` (run): `node scripts/check-references.mjs` and `node --test scripts/test/pilot-agents.test.mjs` green; the selection green.

Rewrite check: any wording that keeps a rule's meaning and its reason keeps all four.
Misses: whether a reworded rule still steers the agent; the security reviewer reads every cut and rewording (A4).
