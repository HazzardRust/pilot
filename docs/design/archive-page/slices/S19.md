# S19 Rows and bar aligned and grouped (added)

Feature `archive-page` · delivers spec 14 · source: `../architecture.md` §2 (spec 6, 14) · after: S18 (`regions/archive.rs`)
Reviewers: `pilot:rust-reviewer`
Model: opus — a layout judgement the user will look at, across both archive regions.

The user: "this has to be one of the most muddled UIs ive ever seen ... get some alignment and better grouping sorted out. everything feels unpredictable."

What is unpredictable today (`regions/archive.rs` at the feature's tip): a row is one flowing line of kind caption, time caption and title, so the title starts at a different x on every row (kind labels differ in width); the fields flow in a wrapping row of their own with no indent; the sort toggles sit at the end of the search line and the 14 kind toggles wrap below it with no label; day headings and their counts float. The detail (`regions/archive_detail.rs`) lists labels and values without a column.

## Files owned

- `deck/rune/src/regions/archive.rs`: rows as fixed columns, the bar's groups.
- `deck/rune/src/regions/archive_detail.rs`: the detail's labels as one column.
- `deck/rune/tests/deck/archive_align.rs` (new) and its `mod` line in `deck/rune/tests/deck/main.rs`.

## Behaviours

1. `row columns line up` (Rust test, layout rects from a frame): over rows of three kinds with labels of different widths, every row's kind, time and title start at the same x as every other row's, at 1280 and 900 wide; kind and time columns are fixed widths in `ch` (the longest kind label, the time format).
2. `fields sit under the title` (Rust test): a row's fields line starts at its title's x and wraps there.
3. `the bar is grouped` (Rust test): the bar reads as labelled groups in a fixed order: search (full width), then `Sort` with Date, Kind, Title, then `Kinds` with the toggles; each group's label is a caption keyed `archive-group-<name>`; nothing in a group moves when a toggle turns on (on/off changes colour only).
4. `day headings align with the rows` (Rust test): a heading's text starts at the rows' kind x and its count sits right after it in the quiet colour.
5. `detail labels line up` (Rust test): in a closed ticket's detail, every field value starts at the same x.
6. Spec 4, 6 and 12's pins stay green.
