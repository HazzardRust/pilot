# S5 A ticket over the bound is split, and the user chooses one run, sequence or parallel

Feature `run-chaining` · delivers spec 1, 3 (A4's ticket form), 10 · source: `../architecture.md` §2, A3, A4 · after: S4

## Files owned

- `agents/pilot-architect.md`: the one-run bound and the run split in §8 of the artefact.
- `skills/pilot/SKILL.md`: step 3 (the split question) and "The artefact is the state" (a run's ticket form).
- `scripts/test/run-split.test.mjs` (new): the pins.

## Interface

```
agents/pilot-architect.md, the artefact's §8:
  "Over 6 slices (A3), group them into runs of at most 6, each a shippable feature PR with its own Gate 2,
   as a Runs table: run | slices | spec lines | after (a run, or – when it can go on another deck)."
  And send main the split as a stashed decision like any other.
skills/pilot/SKILL.md, step 3, first of the grilling when §8 has a Runs table:
  one `question` on a fresh thread: the Runs table, and the options
  "One run" | "In sequence on this deck" (recommended) | "In parallel on separate decks"
  within the bound: nothing asked
  on "In sequence": after Gate 1, one `ticket` per run 2..k, in order
ticket body for a run of a split (read by S1's next-run frame):
  "Run <n> of <feature>: artefact <path> approved at Gate 1 (<PR>); slices <S…>; spec lines <…>"
```

## Tests

1. `the architect groups more than 6 slices into runs`: `pilot-architect.md` states the bound and the Runs table.
2. `the pilot asks the split once, and only over the bound`: SKILL.md step 3 names the three options and the
   within-bound silence.
3. `a later run's ticket names the artefact, its slices and spec lines`: the ticket-body form in SKILL.md.
4. `the pilot share stays at or under 9800`: the existing pin.

## Done

- **Expected**: a DAG of 8 slices comes back from the architect as two runs, and the pilot's first grilling item
  is the split question. A DAG of 3 slices asks nothing.
- **Verification**: `npm test`; `node scripts/measure-prompt.mjs`.
- **Evidence**: pins red before, green after; the rendered question item seen on the live deck (posted by hand
  from the SKILL.md wording, three options, one recommended).
- **Prohibited**: raising the pin; editing step 1 or Models (housekeeping-audit); a new item kind for the split.
- **Diff limit**: at most +70 tokens on the pilot share; the architect's file is outside it but under +40 lines.
- **Done when**: tests 1–4 are green and the question has been seen on the deck.
