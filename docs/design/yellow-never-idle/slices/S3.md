## Behaviours

1. `one line per idle episode` (test): `noteIdle` → 1 line at 20 min under `t.mock.timers`; still 1 at 60 min with no new heartbeat; a heartbeat then 20 more quiet min gives a 2nd.
2. `a heartbeat defers the fire` (test): `armIdle` → no line at 20 min after a crew heartbeat at 10 min; 1 line at 30 min.
3. `a parked wait wakes` (test): `noteIdle` → a real `wait` on a deck on a free port exits printing the line within 1 poll.

Rewrite check: a sweep interval instead of one-shot timers keeps 1-3.
Misses: how the line reads on the ticket's card; QA looks at it on the scratch deck.
