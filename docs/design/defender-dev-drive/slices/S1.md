## Tests

1. `the row alone matches doctor's row`: `main` → under one faked `run`, `args ['defender']` logs exactly the Defender lines that `args []` logs.
2. `each verdict renders and exits`: `defenderRow` → through `main`, pass exits 0, fail (`not-excluded`) exits 1 and cannot tell (`unknown`) exits 0.
3. `the exclusion leads`: `defenderRow` → a non-pass row's `steps[0]` is the `Add-MpPreference` line and `steps[1]` the Dev Drive line.
