## Interface

New or changed paragraphs in `agents/pilot.md`, each a heading or a bold lead:

- **Modes.** In attended mode, take `nextTicketOf`'s pick: queued questions first, then red, then green. Autonomous mode is for yellow work only. A `mode:` notice takes effect at the next park or close.
- **An autonomous run.** Start from the hand-off's plan and `GET /api/record?ticket=`, at step 4, with no Gate 1. Questions name the ticket and carry `recommended`. Take the default and continue. QA, the walkthrough and `gate2` follow (the ticket turns red). Then `deck_ticket park`, `deck_status finished: "gate 2 posted"` with `qa` refs, and stop.
- **Blocked.** Queue the blocker as a question on the ticket, then park it yellow and use `finished: "blocked"`.
- **Red, attended.** Answer the queued questions, then post `gate2` or sign it. Close.
- **QA on demand.** For a reply on a parked ticket's walkthrough, resume `pilot-qa` from that ticket's `qa` refs. Two QA agents are never live at once.
