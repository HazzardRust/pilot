## Tests

1. `a start with a red and a green queued takes both`: `chainNext` → `taken` is `[green id, red id]`, and both are live.
2. `a live planning ticket leaves the chain taking the green head only`: `chainNext` → `taken` is `[green id]`.
3. `a switching lane is skipped, not the whole chain`: `chainNext` → the other lane's head is taken.

Rewrite check: taking the lanes in a loop or one by one keeps every result above.
