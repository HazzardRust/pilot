## Tests

1. `moveTicket up swaps a ticket with the open ticket above it in the same repository`: queue order after, and after a re-read of the store.
2. `a move skips another repository's tickets and closed tickets`: interleaved ALPHA/BETA and a taken ticket; only ALPHA's open order changes.
3. `the head refuses up and the tail refuses down`: `DeckRefusal`, store unchanged.
4. `moving a non-ticket or a closed ticket is refused`.
5. `after a move the chain takes the new head`: `chainNext(ALPHA)` hands off the moved ticket.
6. `POST /move from the page reorders and redirects; a foreign Origin is refused`: via `deckOnAPort`.
7. `the tab renders arrows, none up on the head and none down on the tail`: `renderHomePage`/tab render.
