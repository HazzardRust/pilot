## Tests

1. `a ticket needed later is the green tail`: `enterStage` → after `needTicket` on the older yellow, `ticketQueueOf(…, 'green')` ends with it, and `nextTicketOf(…, 'green')` is the other green.
2. `a gate2 post on a yellow is the green tail`: `enterStage` → the same order after `postItem` of a gate2 for the older yellow.
3. `a gate2 on a green keeps its place`: `enterStage` → `listItems()` ids identical before and after.
4. `other items keep their order`: `enterStage` → with a question and another repo's green between, every id other than the ticket keeps its relative order, and the other repo's queue is unchanged.
5. `a lone green stays where it was`: `enterStage` → with no green in the repo, `deck.items` order unchanged.

Rewrite check: replacing the splice with any reordering that keeps deck order the queue keeps all five results, so long as `ticketQueueOf` and `nextTicketOf` read the same.
