## Behaviours

1. `a line off the caret renders bold, italic and code` (test): `preview::spans` → on `"**b** _i_ `c`\nx"` with the caret on line 2, a bold span over `b`, an italic span over `i`, a mono span with a fill over `c`, quiet spans over each marker.
2. `the caret's line stays raw and renders once the caret leaves` (test): `preview::spans` → caret on line 1: 0 spans in line 1's bytes; caret moved to line 2: line 1's spans present, 0 in line 2's.
3. `a selection keeps every line it touches raw, and clips at a char boundary` (test): `preview::spans` → a selection over lines 1-2 of 3 leaves spans on line 3 only; a span crossing a raw line with `é` ends on a char boundary.
4. `an unfocused box renders every line` (test): `preview::spans` → `focused = false`: spans on every line.
5. `the sent text is the typed text` (test): `reply::Local::send` → after typing `"**a**\n# b"` and moving the caret, the posted `Action::Reply.text` equals it byte for byte.
6. `the box shows it` (look): `--snap` of the thread screen with a two-line draft; the live window on a scratch deck.

Rewrite check: any preview that styles by decoration and keeps the draft untouched keeps 2 and 5.
Misses: how faint markers read on both themes (A1), and IME composition; QA's walkthrough reads them.
