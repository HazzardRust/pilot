## Behaviours

1. `crew runs in a temp config` (read): `probes/crew/run.mjs` spawns `claude` with `env: scratchEnv(...)` (temp `CLAUDE_CONFIG_DIR`, `CLAUDE*`/`PILOT_*`/`ANTHROPIC*` dropped), and its allow is `Bash(node <its io.mjs> *)`, not `Bash(node *)`.
2. `a locked copy is still removed` (read): `scrub()` deletes the credential copy only through the retrying recursive `rmSync` inside its `try`; an `exit` handler throw cannot skip the remaining dirs.
3. `no copy is left, checked` (run): after its runs each credentialed runner scans `os.tmpdir()` for its prefix, prints `left: <n>` and exits 1 when n > 0; one `node probes/sections/run.mjs` run prints `left: 0`.
4. `crew cleans its root; restart ends its tree` (read): crew removes its scratch root after its row; restart's 30 s and 40 s timers call `endChild(child)`.

Rewrite check: any runner that passes the shared scratch env and scans for left copies passes 1-3.
Misses: a SIGKILL of the runner itself; the next run's `sweep()` takes it.

## Done

- **Expected**: L8's terms hold in all three probes, checked by the runner, not by hand.
- **Verification**: `npm test`; behaviour 3's run.
- **Evidence**: the diff and the `left: 0` line.
- **Prohibited**: no change to a probe's measurement; no credential in any output.
- **Done when**: 1-4 hold and the gate is green, under 150 changed lines.
