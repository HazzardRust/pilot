## Tests

1. `a blocking walkthrough is due at 20 minutes`: `yieldsDue` → one entry at 20 min, none at 19.
2. `a non-blocking walkthrough never is`: `yieldsDue` → none.
3. `a walkthrough on a red or green ticket never is`: `yieldsDue` → none.
