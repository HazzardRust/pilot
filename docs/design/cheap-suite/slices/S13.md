## Found

`deck/test/co-pilot-agent.test.mjs` is red on ubuntu-latest only, a different test each run: `a co-pilot heartbeat after the grant stamps the entry` and `nothing else stamps it` (run 45193025742, `co-pilot-agent.test.mjs:36:10`, `false !== true`), `a worktree heartbeat counts for its repository` (run 45190352202, `:86:10`, `false !== true`) while the other two passed. A red that moves between runs is not a path rule.

The cause is the clock, not the path: `noteCoPilotAgent` (`deck/store-tickets.mjs:566`) stamps only a heartbeat strictly later than the grant, `!(now > Date.parse(entry.at))`, by design (`nothing else stamps it` asserts `a heartbeat at the grant itself` is refused). The fixture's `granted()` grants and the test heartbeats on the next line; on a fast runner both fall in one millisecond and the stamp is refused. `path.join('D:', 'work', 'pilot')` is relative on Linux, but `repoOf` falls back to the string and `repoKey` keys grant and heartbeat alike.
